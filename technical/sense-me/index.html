Sense Me

A project from my earlier engineering notebook. Original descriptions, resources and demonstrations are preserved below.

one curiosity
leads to another
Download APK

An android app to put smartphone automatically in silent mode.

A user needs to mark points on the map to identify areas where their phones will be silent.
Once marked, the background activity tracks the GPS movement of the phone.

When the phone is inside the market area, it puts the phone in silent and removes the silent profile when outside or after a set time.
