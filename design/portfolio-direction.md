**Hero:** “I turn LLMs into working systems.”

**Supporting copy:** “I’m Gokul, a product and engineering architect. I design LLM and vision-led agents, the context they work with, and the evaluations that tell us how they behave.”

## Personal story

1. **2016–2019 / Flytta:** on-device YOLOv2 and LSTM inference, plus co-founding and building a company.
2. **2020–2021 / Pramati:** LEGAL-BERT, knowledge graphs, entity linking and audio-visual deep learning.
3. **2021–2023 / Accenture Labs:** RAG, transformers, document intelligence, physics-informed ML and large data systems.
4. **2023–2025 / Plena Data:** five specialized agents, context continuity, workflow design and a 13-engineer team.
5. **2025–present / LaunchAI:** enterprise agents, vision-first automation, MCP/tool standards, model evaluation and product direction.

This progression is more credible than a wall of framework logos or an unqualified “LLM expert” badge.

## Page progression

1. **Introduction:** specialty and a clear first-person promise, followed by a large centered digital portrait. Show name, location, current role and direct contact.
2. **Selected work:** two detailed agent case studies. Each separates problem, own contribution, architecture themes and scoped outcomes. LaunchAI’s “3 months → 2 weeks” is time to market, not inference latency. Plena’s “5 agents” describes that platform, not every project.
3. **How I think about systems:** an interactive conceptual sequence: task → context → action → evaluation. Explicitly a personal design framework, not a disclosed company implementation or a live agent demo.
4. **Skills through the work:** an interactive career timeline. Connect capabilities to roles, dated evidence and relevant links.
5. **Built and shared:** Refuse.dev, slm.expert, Agentend.ai and the existing “Small Models, Big Impact” talk. Do not invent blog posts to fill a writing section.
6. **The person:** hockey, Python/AI communities, Chennai and Computer Science studies. Proposed personal copy should stay within those supplied facts.
7. **Contact:** direct email and LinkedIn, with a concrete invitation to discuss an AI product or agent system.

## Visual direction

Warm off-white, near-black text, restrained cobalt accents, oversized editorial headings and generous spacing. A serif italic is an occasional human accent. Thin rules and numbered sections give structure. The avatar is centered and large. Technical material uses restrained diagrams and compact monospace annotations, without fake terminals or simulated activity.

Interactions serve the content: case-study disclosures, architecture steps, timeline selection and the existing cursor-aware portrait. Subtle entrance motion respects reduced-motion preferences. Avoid skill percentages, badge walls, fake execution logs and repeated SaaS feature-card grids.

The concept is a separate local preview at `/expert-portfolio.html`; it does not replace the current homepage or publish the résumé attachment. The current 3D renderer is reused, not changed by this design pass.
